A 6×6 board, one number in hand. Pick a column, the number falls, and three of a kind that touch become the next one up. Chains pay more. Climb to 35 — then keep going.

Free, in your browser — no account, nothing to install. The iPhone and Android apps are on their way.

  1. Three of the same number, touching, become the next one up. Four or five still make one; six make two.
  2. Whatever was above a merge falls into the gap and can set off the next — a chain, and each link pays more.
  3. Merges charge the bomb; a blast clears the eight squares around it. Infinity falls in now and then: set it off, name a number, and every one of that number goes.
  4. The colours climb with you, light to dark, and 35 wears every one of them. The board fills; the run ends. Beat your best.

Nothing leaves your device: the game keeps your run and your best score in your own browser, and nowhere else.
