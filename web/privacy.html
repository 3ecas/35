Last updated 6 October 2026

35 collects no data. Not about you, not about how you play. There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, no advertising and no analytics. The game does not use the network at all: everything it needs is inside it.

What the game keeps

So that a run can be picked up where it was left, the game keeps a few things on your own device and nowhere else:

  • the run in progress — the board, the number in hand, the score and the bomb's charge;
  • your best score;
  • whether sound is on, and whether you have seen how to play.

In the browser this lives in the site's local storage; in the apps, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes all of it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and, since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If the game ever does more than this — adds advertising, say — this page will say so before it does, and the store listings will be updated to match.
